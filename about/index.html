A different lens
on discovery.

The Science of Science & Computational Discovery Lab studies how research happens—and develops computational methods to improve it.

Led by Professor Daniel Acuña in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Colorado Boulder, we mine scientific knowledge from large, unstructured collections of publications, citations, and images.

Our methods include deep learning, natural language processing, graph analytics, image processing, and causal inference.

Many paths.
A shared starting point.

Our alumni have gone on to work as software engineers, data scientists, data engineers, and security analysts, as well as postdoctoral researchers and assistant professors.

At home in Boulder.
