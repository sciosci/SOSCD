# Replacement direction analysis

Seed: 0f53ec25. Assigned grounded candidate: 4. Mode: persuade on the homepage; read on the archive and supporting pages.

## Audience and mechanism

Scientists and prospective researchers inspect the site on ordinary laptop and phone screens in offices, libraries, or between meetings. A light reading ground fits that scene. The lab applies computation to the scientific record itself, making research practices and knowledge discoverable and examinable.

The category default is an institutional banner, decorative scientific picture, and three navigation cards. Its predictable opposite is a dark neon AI landing page. Neither should define this redesign.

## Seven grounded directions, ordered by resonance

1. Science atlas: cobalt scientific diagrams, clear white pages, a navigable map of published work. Strong immediate identification; risks a familiar modern research-lab look.
2. Peer-review manuscript: precise annotations and highlighted evidence on clean paper; publication excerpts organize the surface. Strong connection to research integrity, weaker for the full research portfolio.
3. Conference poster: confidently oversized geometric sans typography, yellow fields, and a research-question-led composition. Easily read at a distance, but can make the lab feel like a one-time event.
4. Optical research bench: cool white work surface, deeply colored violet instruments and chartreuse active marks; a scientific network becomes an inspectable specimen. The homepage combines large explanatory type with a focused interactive research field; dense pages inherit the exact same optical clarity and controls. Selected by seed.
5. Research field notebook: green functional navigation and highly legible observations, people and projects collected as working notes. Human and useful, but can overstate a fieldwork identity.
6. Scientific wayfinding: a blue information system with strong route labels, diagrammatic paths, and precise navigation. Excellent orientation, less expressive of research practices.
7. Scholarly annotation system: graphite and highlighter yellow, wide margins, tightly set findings and expandable evidence. Useful for reading, but too close to manuscript direction to own the whole identity alone.

These span graphical notation, documentary practice, physical instruments, and public information systems.

## Challenger evaluation

Each judgment compares audience identification and product clarity after translating the source form to actual lab content.

- Console dashboard atmosphere: declined on both axes. Publications as floating memory objects obscure scanning and imply entertainment. Keep its discipline of one dominant spatial focal object; no generic glowing cards.
- Mezzotint velvet night: declined on both axes. Scientific questions could be progressively revealed, but dark print plates poorly support frequent bibliography reading. Keep its strong hierarchy between foreground evidence and quiet background.
- Nixie laboratory counter: declined on both axes. A research counter overemphasizes metrics and physical instrumentation the lab does not claim. Keep precise, consistent numeric typesetting in years and counts.
- HyperCard stack: declined on both axes. Linked cards fit citations, but editable pixel cards imply a retro authoring tool. Keep addressable content and a reliable return path.
- Accretion disk: declined on both axes. A publication orbit can expose connections, but astrophysical imagery misidentifies the lab's domain. Keep continuous visual structure and an explicit selected state in the research field.
- Multiplane dawn: declined on both axes. Boulder scenery could contextualize the lab, but a forest journey obscures the research mechanism. Keep clear depth ordering and a complete static composition when motion is reduced.

## Raised assigned direction

The optical research bench owns one coherent specimen field, clear reading planes, exact year and count typography, directly addressable publications, unmistakable selected states, and a complete motion-free presentation. These are structural lessons from the challengers, not borrowed decorative motifs.

## Proposed experience

Immediately explain the lab's scientific purpose. Put a concrete research exploration beside that explanation, not another generic AI illustration. Continue through research questions, selected publications, real people, concise recent news with the full archive accessible, and a specific invitation to join. Carry the shared typography, violet/white surfaces, chartreuse action color, and navigational clarity through every route.
